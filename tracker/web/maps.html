Response maps

Where each frozen backbone responds, before any fine-tuning

Response map = activity (channel norm) of the last feature map of the frozen SSL backbone, upsampled to the image. No labels and no fine-tuning are involved; the annotation boxes are used only to measure the map. Mass in box = share of the map's total inside the boxes (a map with no preference scores the box area). Peak in box = share of images whose strongest pixel lies inside a box. Bars = mean of the split backbones, dots = splits.

On target-centred chips the in-domain backbones (A1, XI plain DINO) already look at the vehicle. Among the SARDet-trained backbones only RegionDINO object-only is above the ImageNet start; scene+object and every control sit at the plain-DINO level.

On full scenes the ImageNet start is the most object-focused backbone and plain DINO on SARDet spreads its response over the scene. Object-only brings the peak back onto an object three times as often as plain DINO; SparK is the other focused backbone. Mass barely moves because the boxes are small and the map is at stride 32.
