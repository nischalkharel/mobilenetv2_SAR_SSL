The story as it stands, 2026-11-20

Problem. Self-supervised learning on large unlabeled SAR scene archives is the practical setting, but scene-trained backbones transfer to object chips worse than plain ImageNet weights (c03), because they learn the scene rather than the objects (c07).

Method. Cut DINO's views around label-free bright regions. That turns the loss into the best cross-dataset result, +9 points at 1 % labels, with a random-window control showing the placement matters (c04). Keeping the scene views dilutes it (c05).

Limits, honestly measured. It does nothing when the SSL images are already chips (c06), and on an easy in-dataset chip test nothing separates (c09). On detection the crop size is what helps, not the placement (c08).

What is missing for a strong paper. A second scenes-in, chips-out dataset pair confirming c04, and a map of the crop-size effect (c08) so the recipe has a rule behind it. Both are on the to-do list below.
