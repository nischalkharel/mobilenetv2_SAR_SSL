Reading, 2026-11-19

In-domain (SSL and test both on ATRNet chips): any SSL helps a lot at 1 % labels (21 → 35 to 41 %). CondDINO is top but uses acquisition metadata, so it sits apart. Plain DINO and LeJEPA tie. SparK is weakest here.

Cross-dataset (SSL on SARDet scenes, test on ATRNet chips): plain DINO and LeJEPA transfer worse than no SSL. RegionDINO object-only turns that into the best cross-dataset row (+9.4 over plain DINO at 1 %), with SparK second. The random-window control gets a third of the gain, so the regions matter. Cross-dataset is still far below in-domain (27 vs 35 at 1 %).

RegionDINO on the ATRNet chips themselves (arm I): no gain. Scene+object is level with plain DINO, object-only is 2 points below. A chip is already the object; cutting regions out of it only removes context. So the object views pay off when the SSL images are scenes and the test is a chip, and that is the setting to build the paper on.

Detection (SSL and test on SARDet): no method moves mAP by more than a point. SparK is the only one positive at every budget. RegionDINO's detection rows are being computed.

Next: a classification test where the SSL pool is large scenes and the labeled chips come from the same sensor (Sentinel-1 scenes + OpenSARShip chips, or chips cut from SARDet boxes), and later a non-SARDet scene pool (Umbra / Capella). Ideas and doubts go in the idea box.
