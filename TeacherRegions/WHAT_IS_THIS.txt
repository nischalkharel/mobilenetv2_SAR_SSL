TeacherRegions: a big pretrained SAR model says where the objects are; the small model learns there.

What this folder is
-------------------
RegionDINO (RegionSSL/) cuts DINO's views around label-free regions found by a brightness rule (bright compact
blobs). That rule is cheap but can only find bright blobs: faint targets are missed, bright clutter is kept.
Here the regions come instead from the attention / saliency of a large pretrained SAR foundation model (SARMAE,
ViT-B, trained on 1.3 M SAR chips; models/SARMAE/). The student stays MobileNetV2 and the training recipe stays
RegionDINO object-only; only the region file changes. The teacher is frozen, is never fine-tuned, and is not used
at test time: it acts as a filter that tells the self-supervised student what to look at while it learns.

The question in one line
------------------------
Does a teacher-proposed region file beat the brightness rule (and random windows) for the compact student, and is
that gain more than what plain transfer from the teacher (distillation of its features into MobileNetV2) gives?
The comparison that answers "is this just transfer learning": teacher-regions RegionDINO vs feature distillation
from the same teacher vs the brightness-rule RegionDINO, all with MobileNetV2, all tested on the same ATRNet
transfer and SARDet detection protocols.

Which teacher and why
---------------------
SARMAE ViT-B (CVPR 2026): the only recent SAR foundation model with public, ungated weights, trained on the right
sensors and resolutions, with attention maps shown in its paper to sit on objects. Fallback: SARATR-X v1 (HiViT-B,
click-through gate). SARATR-X v2 is stronger but needs an email to the author. Survey in DESIGN.md section 2.
Caveat recorded there: SARMAE's pretraining pool contains SARDet-100K (no labels), not ATRNet-STAR.
