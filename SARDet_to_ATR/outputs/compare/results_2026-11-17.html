Self-supervised learning on SARDet-100K: what transfers

MobileNetV2 from ImageNet weights, then 100 epochs of SSL on 94,034 unlabeled SARDet-100K scenes, then supervised fine-tuning with 1, 3 or 10 % of the labels. Two downstream tests: the same dataset (detection, mAP) and a different one (ATRNet-STAR vehicle chips, 40-class accuracy). Three splits per cell; every difference is paired on the same split and labeled set.

ATRNet-STAR, SSL on SARDet vs no SSL

Scene-level DINO and LeJEPA transfer worse than plain ImageNet

DINO: −3.0 / −1.6 / +0.5 points at 1 / 3 / 10 %. LeJEPA: −8.5 / −10.6 / −7.2. Every split agrees in sign except DINO at 10 %.

SparK is the only method that carries over to another dataset

+1.9 / +4.6 / +2.2 points, positive on all nine split-budget cells, and within 2 points of SparK trained on ATRNet itself.

SparK is positive at every budget (+0.7 / +0.6 / +0.7, 9 of 9 splits). LeJEPA is +1.1 at 1 % but −1.0 at 10 %. DINO and CondDINO are within half a point.

Where the numbers come from

Detection tables: SARDet/compare/compare.py → SARDet/compare/outputs/20261117-223630/ (summary.md, runs.csv, per_source.csv). Model selection: best validation mAP on the 800-image selection set.
SSL and fine-tune runs: CRC A100 40 GB, repository commit b111bda (results), SSL runs at 7aea6de and earlier. SSL export rule: final epoch, no selection. Fine-tune seeds fixed at 0; splits differ in labeled set and SSL seed.
