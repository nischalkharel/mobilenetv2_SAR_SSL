# Recent (Jan 2025 - Nov 2026) SAR representation-learning papers: novelty check against our findings

Scope: papers dated January 2025 to November 2026 only. Search date: 2026-11-25. Our findings being checked: (A) label-free object-centred view selection for SSL on SAR scenes; (B) scene-to-chip transfer of SSL backbones; (C) SSL results on ATRNet-STAR, FAIR-CSAR, chips cut from SARDet-100K; (D) compact backbones (MobileNetV2 class) with SSL on SAR; (E) a SAR foundation model used as a frozen proposer / teacher for a small model.

Sources used: arXiv abstract + HTML pages, Semantic Scholar citation API (citers of ATRNet-STAR arXiv:2501.13354 = 69 papers; citers of SARDet-100K arXiv:2403.06534 = 159 papers; citers of the DOI 10.1109/TGRS.2024.3519891 = 62 papers, which by its content is the FAIR-CSAR citation list but the API did not echo the cited title, so treat that attribution as probable, not confirmed), GitHub READMEs, Europe PMC, and one journal PDF (Science China review) read locally. IEEE Xplore, MDPI, PMC and SPIE full texts were blocked (403 / CAPTCHA / empty page), so for those papers only metadata, abstracts from aggregators, or search snippets are available; this is flagged per item.

## Master list: dated 2025-2026 papers, ordered closest-first to our findings

### Takeaway
The single closest paper is DINOSAR (IJDE, accepted 2026-11-04; Tang et al.): a DINO/iBOT pretraining on 7M SAR tiles whose core contribution is label-free "content-aware multi-crop" view selection, shown to beat random-crop DINO on ATRNet-STAR and SARDet-100K. It overlaps directly with finding (A) but at ViT-S/B scale, on a 7M-sample pool, and without a random-region-placement control, a scene-to-chip ablation, or a compact CNN. Nothing found reproduces (B), (D) or (E) as we frame them; (C) now has several strong published numbers to compare against.

## Q5. Any 2025-2026 paper reporting negative transfer of SAR SSL (below ImageNet) or discussing scene-vs-chip mismatch?

### Takeaway
No paper states "SAR SSL is worse than ImageNet initialization" as a headline, but several tables contain such rows: SARATR-X under LoRA is beaten by optical DINOv2 (71.3 vs 71.7 average, and lowest at every shot count on ATRNet SOC-40); SARATR-X, SAR-JEPA and SARMAE fine-tune below ResNet-18/HDANet on ATRNet-STAR EOC-Scene; fine-tuned DINOv3 is below ImageNet-MAE on FUSAR; SAMBA loses to SARATR-X / SUMMIT on some few-shot cells. Scene-vs-chip mismatch is never analysed; pools simply mix both.

### Cited Findings
- AFRL: nearest-neighbour SARATR-X 44.6 vs ImageNet ResNet-50 26.8 (SAR SSL wins) but LoRA SARATR-X 71.3 vs torch.hub DINOv2 71.7; SARATR-X "is lowest across shots" under LoRA on ATRNet SOC-40; p1 (no ATRNet chips in pool) "trails [optical DINOv2] at >=100 shots"; adding SAMPLE-syn lowered SAMPLE K=0 59.5 -> 57.9 — [arXiv 2509.21722 HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.21722)
- ATRNet-STAR EOC-Scene fine-tune: SARATR-X 13.9, SAR-JEPA 12.6, SARMAE 19.9, DINOSAR-B(SAR-1M) 16.0 vs ResNet-18 16.1, ResNet-34 18.0, VGG16 21.6, HDANet 33.6 — [DINOSAR results](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/kenanking/DINOSAR/main/docs/results.md)
- DINOSAR few-shot MSTAR 10-shot: DINOSAR-B 90.7 vs SARATR-X 95.6 (a 7M-image DINO loses to a 180K MIM on MSTAR few-shot) — [same](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/kenanking/DINOSAR/main/docs/results.md)
- SARMAE: fine-tuned DINOv3 74.25 vs ImageNet-MAE 75.40 on FUSAR-Ship; attributes poor transfer of natural/optical pretraining to the domain gap — [arXiv 2512.16635 HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.16635v2)
- SAMBA: MSTAR 5-shot 95.0 vs SARATR-X 95.9; SAR-ACD 30% 81.1 vs SUMMIT 84.3; supervised Mamba below supervised ViT at all shots — [arXiv 2606.31668 HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.31668v2)
- Background-shortcut paper: SARATR-X relies more on background than ConvNeXt and "does not solve the scene change" — [arXiv 2611.21827](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21827)
- XuanJian ablation: optical DINOv2 baseline already 91.76 on SOC-50; SAR-domain DINO adds +2.25, LC branch +2.69 — [arXiv 2611.06105 PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.06105)
- Pu & Xu: SAR-MAE 54.9 vs ImageNet-MAE 53.6 (positive but +1.3 only) — [arXiv 2501.11249 HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2501.11249)
- Scene/chip composition: AFRL p1 (scenes/ships) 61.6 -> p3 (+68K ATRNet chips) 79.6 NN average — [arXiv 2509.21722 HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.21722); SARATR-X-v2 and XuanJian pool chips, detection images and scenes without ablation — [SARATR-X-v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.23238v4); [XuanJian](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.06105)
- General (non-SAR) remote-sensing context: "ImageNet pre-training remains a competitive baseline" for Sentinel-2 SSL — [ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/389090536_Is_Self-Supervised_Pre-training_on_Satellite_Imagery_Better_than_ImageNet_A_Systematic_Study_with_Sentinel-2)
- Searches tried without a SAR-specific negative-transfer hit: "SAR self-supervised pretraining worse than ImageNet negative transfer"; "SAR self-supervised scene images target chips resolution mismatch pretraining transfer 2026".

### Inferences
- Our arm-A result (SSL on SARDet-100K scenes transfers worse than ImageNet to ATRNet-STAR chips, except SparK) is consistent with these scattered rows and would be the first paper to state scene-to-chip negative transfer explicitly with matched steps and a compact backbone.
- The EOC-Scene rows are the clearest published evidence that SAR MIM foundation models are not better than small supervised CNNs under scene shift; this strengthens the case for region-based views.

### Gaps
- No paper discusses crop-size or resolution mismatch between scene pretraining and chip tasks (the only resolution-related 2026 SSL paper, SparK-SAR, is about feature-map stride on scene tasks).

## Q6. Any 2025-2026 paper on lightweight / edge SAR models trained with SSL?

### Takeaway
None at the MobileNetV2 scale with DINO/JEPA/SparK-style SSL on SAR targets. The nearest are: SAR-SimCLR 11M (AFRL baseline, 60.3 NN avg on 10 chip tasks), DINOSAR ViT-S/16 21.4M (within 1-2 pts of ViT-B after fine-tuning on ATRNet-STAR), SAMBA 27M Mamba, SparK-SAR 27.8M ConvNeXt (scene tasks), a MobileNet-with-pretext-task SSL on SAMPLE (Tuskegee), and supervised KD/NAS compact detectors (LightKD-SAR 11.43M, SAR-NAS, SURGE, Light-ResKAN).

### Cited Findings
- SAR-SimCLR 11M params: 60.3 average NN balanced accuracy vs AFRL-DINOv2-B 79.6 and SARATR-X 44.6 — [arXiv 2509.21722 HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.21722)
- DINOSAR ViT-S/16 (21.4M): SOC-40 k-NN 67.14; fine-tune SOC-40 98.8, SOC-50 90.3; MSTAR 10-shot 89.7 — [DINOSAR results](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/kenanking/DINOSAR/main/docs/results.md)
- SAMBA: ~27M params, ~8 GFLOPs, "over 65% reduction versus Transformer baselines"; no edge deployment test — [arXiv 2606.31668 HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.31668v2)
- SparK-SAR: 27.8M ConvNeXt-RS, beats 139M Copernicus-FM on 3/4 S1 tasks — [arXiv 2610.21993](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21993)
- Tuskegee Sensors: nine pretext tasks, SAMPLE, MobileNet among classifiers; snippet numbers MobileNet 98.70%, MobileNet-0.25 92.21% (unverified) — [Europe PMC](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/search?query=PMCID:PMC12787659&resultType=core&format=json); [DOI](https://doi.org/10.3390/s26010122)
- LightKD-SAR: 11.43M / 15.7 GFLOPs, 50.92 mAP SARDet-100K, supervised teacher — [DOAJ](https://doaj.org/article/32055ce874b248ab8beee9fb3f63cfdd)
- SURGE contrastive distillation: +6.2 mAP on SSDD/HRSID students — [arXiv 2605.30380](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.30380)
- Light-ResKAN: MSTAR 99.09, 163.78x fewer params than VGG16, supervised — [arXiv 2604.01903](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.01903)
- Other lightweight supervised titles in citer lists: "Lightweight SAR Ship Detection via Pearson Correlation and Nonlocal Distillation" (GRSL 2025, DOI 10.1109/LGRS.2025.3545569); CARL-RTDETR (IEEE TAP 2026); LW-ResUNet (GRSL 2026, DOI 10.1109/LGRS.2026.3703955); TVLightFormer (Remote Sensing 2026) — [SARDet-100K citers](https://api.semanticscholar.org/graph/v1/paper/arXiv:2403.06534/citations?fields=title,year,venue,externalIds,publicationDate&limit=300)
- Non-SAR template for frozen-FM-to-edge distillation: Foundry (CVPR 2026, 3D), MuCoDi (pathology, MobileOne/RepViT students) — [arXiv 2511.20721](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2511.20721); [arXiv 2607.05533](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.05533)
- A ResearchSquare preprint titled "Lightweight Self-Supervised Representation Learning with Knowledge Distillation on Compact Datasets" (MobileNetV2 teacher, EfficientNet-B0 student) is on STL-10, not SAR — irrelevant despite surfacing in search — [preprint](https://assets-eu.researchsquare.com/files/rs-6866114/v1_covered_2ea83d0f-559c-4d20-969f-57b69f638f05.pdf?c=1752808599)

### Inferences
- The compact-model + SAR-SSL + target-chip combination is open. The one data point at ~11M (SAR-SimCLR) shows a big gap to ViT-B DINOv2 (60.3 vs 79.6 NN), which is exactly the gap a "SAR foundation model as teacher for a small model" paper would target; nobody has published that.
- DINOSAR ViT-S vs ViT-B (67.1 vs 77.0 k-NN, but 98.8 vs 99.1 fine-tuned) suggests the small-model penalty is mostly in frozen-feature quality, which is where distillation from a frozen SAR FM would act.
